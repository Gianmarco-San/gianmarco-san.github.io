VO₂max Estimator

Jack Daniels & Gilbert (1979) formula · based on race performance
%VO₂max = 0.8 + 0.1894·e−0.012778·t + 0.2990·e−0.1933·t (t in min) · VO₂ = −4.60 + 0.1823·v + 0.000104·v² (v in m/min) · VO₂max = VO₂ / %VO₂max. Classifications are indicative age-adjusted norms.

Race Time Predictor

Riegel formula (1977) — enter a Personal Best, predict all distances

Formula: T₂ = T₁ × (D₂/D₁)1.06. The exponent 1.06 models increasing fatigue over longer distances. Most accurate for distances within ~3× of the reference; less reliable when comparing sprints to marathons. Reference distance row is highlighted.
